Privacy Policy

Mindra Mosaic Publisher · Last updated 21 November 2026

Mindra Mosaic Publisher ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the Mindra Mosaic channel. It is used only by its operator to upload and schedule the operator's own videos to the operator's own Facebook Page. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about people who view, like or comment on those videos.

Information the app uses

When the operator signs in with Facebook, the app receives, through Meta's official login:

  • the operator's Facebook user ID and name;
  • the ID, name, link and profile picture of the Facebook Pages the operator chooses to grant;
  • access tokens that let the app publish videos and Reels to those Pages.

The permissions requested are pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts. The app does not request or receive friends lists, messages, email addresses, payment details or any data about other Facebook users.

How the information is used

  • To list the operator's Pages so the correct Page can be selected.
  • To upload, schedule and publish the operator's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to that Page.
  • To read basic results of those posts (such as their status and view counts) for the operator's own reporting.

The information is never sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Data is stored only on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted. No data is sent anywhere except to Meta's Graph API, over HTTPS, to perform the actions above.

Retention

Tokens and Page details are kept only while the Page is connected. Disconnecting the Page in the app deletes its stored access token immediately.

Data deletion

To delete all data the app holds about you:

  1. Remove the app in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select the app and choose Remove. This revokes all of its access tokens at once.
  2. Or email the contact address below asking for deletion. Stored tokens and Page details are deleted within 7 days and the deletion is confirmed by reply.

Videos already published to a Page belong to that Page and can be deleted by a Page admin in Meta Business Suite.

Changes
